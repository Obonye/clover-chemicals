# Clover Industries: Usability & UX Audit

**Method.** Followed the impeccable `audit` playbook (5 technical dimensions, P0–P3 severity) with a usability lens on the key journeys: *find a product → request a quote*, *contact the company*, and *move between the Chemical and Medical divisions*. The review is source-level, covering every route in `app/` and every component in `components/`. WCAG contrast ratios were computed from the real `oklch` tokens in `styles/globals.css`.

**Limits.**
- The impeccable deterministic detector (`impeccable detect`) was **not run**: the session's permission policy blocked running the downloaded binary. There are no detector findings to reconcile. Everything below is manual, verified against source.
- There was no live browser pass, so touch-gesture behaviour and real layout at 375px are inferred from code, not observed.
- Sanity content was not queried, so claims about which category slugs exist rely on the code comments in `config/site.ts`.

## Implementation Integrity Verdict: **FAIL**

The visual system is coherent, but the site doesn't do what it claims. The main job of a B2B chemical site is to capture enquiries, and that path is broken:

- **The contact form never sends anything.** `app/contact/page.tsx:20-25` calls `preventDefault()`, then `setTimeout(() => setFormState("success"), 800)` with `// TODO: wire up to an email service`. Visitors see "Thank you — we'll be in touch" and the enquiry is lost. None of the inputs has a `name` attribute either, so wiring up a backend will also need the fields changed.
- **Every "Spec Sheet" link is dead.** In `components/product-card.tsx:108-114` and `components/medical-product-card.tsx:47-52` it is `href="#"`. It appears on every product card, catalogue-wide, and does nothing when clicked.
- **Links that 404** (no route or file exists):
  - `/news/[slug]`: every "Read more" on `/news`. There's no `app/news/[slug]`.
  - `/careers`, `/privacy`, `/terms`: footer (`components/footer.tsx:9-15`).
  - `/brochure.pdf`: the Brochure quick link on every page (`components/quick-links-bar.tsx:21`). `public/` has no PDF.
  - `/products/PPE` and `/products/pool`: footer Products column. The comment in `config/site.ts:13-16` already records that these are stale.
- **The site contradicts itself on company facts:**
  - The hero and About say "100% Citizen-Owned". The pillars in `heritage-section.tsx:12-13` and `about/page.tsx:16-17` say "**Majority** citizen-owned". The company profile says 100%.
  - "8 Divisions" appears in the stats, but the site presents two (Chemical, Medical).
- **Placeholder content presented as real.** `/news` shows dated articles such as "Clover Chemical Expands Operations into Zambia" with Unsplash stock photos. Zambia doesn't appear in the company profile. `/blog`, `/docs` and `/pricing` are unstyled template stubs that are still publicly routable.

Each of these alone is fixable. Together they mean a buyer who tries to act (download a spec, read an article, send an enquiry) hits a dead end on almost every path.

## Detailed Findings by Severity

### P0: Blocking

**[P0] Contact / quote form is a mock and loses every enquiry**
- **Location:** `app/contact/page.tsx:20-25`, and all inputs at lines 81–153 (no `name` attributes)
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** Every "Get a Quote", "Request a Quote", "Quote" and "Contact Us" CTA on the site ends here. The user sees a success message, and nobody at Clover ever gets the enquiry. The promise "Our technical team responds within one business day" is then broken, which is worse than having no form at all.
- **Recommendation:** Wire the form to a server action or route handler with a real email provider. Add `name` attributes. Show a real error state on failure, and keep a visible fallback ("or email sales@…") beside the button. Until that ships, replace the form with mailto/WhatsApp/phone CTAs.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### P1: Major

**[P1] Dead "Spec Sheet" link on every product card**
- **Location:** `components/product-card.tsx:108-114`, `components/medical-product-card.tsx:47-52`
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** For regulated chemicals (HCl, glutaraldehyde, IPA), the SDS/spec sheet is often the first thing a buyer or safety officer needs. A link that goes nowhere, repeated dozens of times, erodes trust quickly. The earlier critique (`.impeccable/critique/…best-sellers`) flagged the missing SDS affordance, and now it's there but fake.
- **Recommendation:** Add a `specSheet` file field in Sanity and render the link only when a file exists. Otherwise show "Request SDS", which pre-fills the enquiry.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] ~10 internal links 404**
- **Location:** `app/news/page.tsx:146,185` (`/news/${slug}`, no route); `components/footer.tsx:9,13-14` (`/careers`, `/privacy`, `/terms`); `components/quick-links-bar.tsx:21` (`/brochure.pdf`, missing); `config/site.ts:44,50` (`/products/PPE`, `/products/pool`, used by the footer)
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** These dead ends sit in persistent chrome (footer, floating quick-links bar), so every page carries them. There's also no custom `app/not-found.tsx`, so users land on Next's bare default 404 with no way back.
- **Recommendation:** Remove or hide links until their targets exist. Build `news/[slug]` or make the cards non-links. Source footer categories from Sanity like the navbar does. Add a branded `not-found.tsx` with search and a route to Products and Contact.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Mobile menu is missing About, News, Contact and Get a Quote**
- **Location:** `components/navbar.tsx:131-140` (`mobileNavItems`) and `:343-381`
- **Category:** Responsive / Usability
- **Impact:** On desktop the nav has Products, About Us, News, Contact Us, **Get a Quote** and Browse. Under `lg`, the hamburger shows only product categories, Clover Medical, a theme toggle and Browse Products. A phone user has no nav route to Contact or a quote, and can only find it by scrolling to the footer. The Medical-division mobile menu is thinner still: a single "Product Lines" link.
- **Recommendation:** Build the mobile list from the same source as desktop (`siteConfig.navItems` plus categories), and put "Get a Quote" as the primary button in the drawer.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Accent-green used for small text fails AA (3.03:1)**
- **Location:** `--accent: oklch(0.62 0.19 127)` on `--background`. It's used as text for eyebrows (`hero-chemicals.tsx:27`, `heritage-section.tsx:35,91`, `contact/page.tsx:34`), "View all →" links (`navbar.tsx:254`, `footer.tsx:77`), product-card category and Quote button (`product-card.tsx:66,101`), required-field asterisks and "Clear filters".
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum). Small text needs 4.5:1.
- **Impact:** The brand colour carries meaning (category, CTA, required-field marks) but is hard to read, especially at the 10–12px mono sizes it's usually paired with.
- **Recommendation:** Add an `--accent-text` token (the same hue, L ≈ 0.45–0.50) for text on light surfaces, and keep `--accent` for fills. Medical blue passes at 4.55:1, so only the chemical green needs this.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Opacity-derived grey text is far below AA across footer, partners and product grade**
- **Location:** `footer.tsx:45,54,61,88,107,136,143` (`foreground/25–/40` → **1.76–2.6:1**); `partners-strip.tsx:29` (`/25` → **1.76:1**); `product-card.tsx:81` grade (`/30`, 10px → **2.0:1**); `heritage-section.tsx:61` (`/35` → 2.29:1); `contact/page.tsx:12` placeholder (`/30` → 2.0:1); `medical-partners.tsx:79` (`/30`)
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **WCAG:** 1.4.3
- **Impact:** The legal links, copyright, footer section headings, partner names (a key trust signal) and chemical **grade** (the most important B2B spec on the card) are close to invisible. Grade at 2.0:1 in 10px mono effectively hides the one thing a technical buyer checks first.
- **Recommendation:** Replace ad-hoc `/NN` opacities with the `text-muted` token (4.24:1, which also needs a small bump to reach 4.5). Make the grade `text-foreground` at 12px or larger.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Quote CTAs lose product context**
- **Location:** `product-card.tsx:99-105`, `medical-product-card.tsx:41-46`, and the static category list in `contact/page.tsx:130-140`
- **Category:** Usability
- **Impact:** A buyer clicks "Quote" on *Glyphosate 360 SL, 20 L*, lands on a blank form, and has to remember and retype the product, grade and pack size. The "Product Interest" select is built from the **static** `lib/products.ts` categories, not Sanity, so it can drift from the live catalogue.
- **Recommendation:** Link to `/contact?product=<slug>` and pre-fill the message and select. Source the category options from Sanity.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Contradictory company facts**
- **Location:** `heritage-section.tsx:12-13` and `about/page.tsx:16-17` ("Majority citizen-owned") vs `hero-chemicals.tsx:28` and `about/page.tsx:114` ("100% citizen-owned"). "8 Divisions" at `heritage-section.tsx:6` and `about/page.tsx:9` vs the two divisions the site presents.
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** Citizen ownership and EDD status are procurement-qualifying claims for government tenders. Saying it two ways on the same page invites doubt.
- **Recommendation:** Use "100% citizen-owned" (it matches the company profile) everywhere. Either label "8 divisions" as *business units* and list them, or drop the stat.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Product-lines accordion hides content behind hover**
- **Location:** `components/products-section.tsx:66-207` (desktop), `:210-327` (mobile/tablet)
- **Category:** Accessibility / Responsive
- **WCAG:** 2.1.1 Keyboard (equivalent function), 1.4.13 Content on Hover
- **Impact:** Category descriptions are `opacity: 0` until `onMouseEnter`. Keyboard focus doesn't expand a tile, there's no `:focus-visible` style, and on touch the first tap fires hover and then navigates immediately, so the description is never seen. With 6 tiles at rest, each tile is roughly 213px wide on a 1280px screen, and labels compete with the dithered image. It looks good, but it's the homepage's main navigation into the catalogue.
- **Recommendation:** Mirror hover with `onFocus`/`onBlur` and add a visible focus ring. Show descriptions at rest on the mobile variant (they already fit), and give `.product-row` a `@media (hover: none)` resting state.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] 652 KB logo SVG loaded on every page, twice**
- **Location:** `public/clover-logo.svg` (652 KB), used by `navbar.tsx:188-192` (`<img>`), `footer.tsx:40` (`<img>`) and `footer.tsx:21-30` (CSS background watermark)
- **Category:** Performance
- **Impact:** A 36×36 logo weighs more than the hero photo (`clover large.jpg`, 420 KB). It's almost certainly an embedded raster inside an SVG. On Botswana mobile networks this adds noticeable load time and data cost to every first visit.
- **Recommendation:** Re-export as a real vector (under 10 KB) or a 2× PNG/WebP. The footer watermark can reuse the same optimised file.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`

### P2: Minor

**[P2] Product search makes a server round-trip on every keystroke**
- **Location:** `components/product-catalogue.tsx:97-109`
- **Category:** Performance
- **Impact:** `router.replace()` with new `searchParams` re-renders the dynamic `/products` server component, which re-runs `ALL_PRODUCTS_QUERY` and `CATEGORIES_QUERY`, for each character typed, even though filtering is already client-side. It can cause input jank and wasted Sanity/API calls. The effect also fires once on mount.
- **Recommendation:** Update the URL with `window.history.replaceState` (Next 14.1+ syncs it with `useSearchParams`), or debounce it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`

**[P2] Floating quick-links: small targets, overlapping content, focusable while hidden**
- **Location:** `components/quick-links-bar.tsx:49-83`
- **Category:** Responsive / Accessibility
- **Impact:** The mobile strip has 36×36px targets (below 44px) pinned mid-screen over the right edge of content, including carousel cards and form fields. When it slides away it gets `aria-hidden`, but the links stay tabbable, so focus goes to invisible controls. The desktop bar is 32px tall with 11px text, and it permanently covers the bottom 32px of the viewport.
- **Recommendation:** Use 44px targets. Add `inert` (not just `aria-hidden`) when hidden. Consider a bottom-anchored strip on mobile instead of a mid-edge rail.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Small tap targets across key controls**
- **Location:** product-card "Quote" (`px-2.5 py-1`, ~26px tall), carousel dots (`h-2`, 8px, `medical-product-carousel.tsx:245-252`), utility-bar division links (11px text in a 32px bar)
- **Category:** Responsive
- **WCAG:** 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum, 24px). The first two are near or below it, and all are below the 44px guideline.
- **Recommendation:** Pad hit areas with pseudo-elements, and make the card's primary CTA a full-width 44px button on mobile.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Two competing category experiences**
- **Location:** Nav dropdown → `/products?category=slug` (`navbar.tsx:22-23`). Homepage accordion, product-card image and footer → `/products/[slug]` (a separate landing page with its own tab bar).
- **Category:** Usability / IA
- **Impact:** The same "Agricultural Chemicals" click lands on two different page designs depending on where it came from. Filters, search and sort exist on one and not the other. On the category page, the card image links to the page you're already on.
- **Recommendation:** Pick one canonical destination, or make the category landing page a clear lead-in with a "Filter all products" hand-off. Make the card's product name the link target, not the image.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

**[P2] Missing escape and keyboard handling on the Products dropdown and mobile menu**
- **Location:** `navbar.tsx:155-168`, `:217-264`, `:343-381`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **Impact:** Neither the dropdown nor the mobile menu closes on `Escape`. Outside-click uses `mousedown` only, so touch-and-keyboard paths are inconsistent. There's no skip-to-content link, so keyboard users tab through utility bar, logo and 6+ nav items on every page.
- **Recommendation:** Add an Escape handler, return focus to the trigger, and add a skip link before `<UtilityBar/>`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Auto-scrolling partner marquee with no pause control**
- **Location:** `partners-strip.tsx:25`, `globals.css:168-185`
- **Category:** Accessibility
- **WCAG:** 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide (motion running more than 5s)
- **Impact:** The reduced-motion preference is honoured (good), but other users can only pause it on hover, which isn't available on touch or keyboard. Six names don't need a marquee. At 1.76:1 they're barely visible anyway.
- **Recommendation:** Use a static wrapped row of real partner logos at readable contrast.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`

**[P2] No per-page titles**
- **Location:** Only `app/layout.tsx` and `products/[category]/page.tsx` define metadata.
- **Category:** Usability / SEO
- **Impact:** About, Contact, News, Products and Medical all show "Clover Industries" in the tab, history and bookmarks, and screen readers announce the same title on every navigation.
- **Recommendation:** Export `metadata` from each route's `layout.tsx` (the `%s - Clover Industries` template is already set up).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Bare error page and publicly reachable template stubs**
- **Location:** `app/error.tsx` (unstyled `<h2>` and `<button>`), `app/blog`, `app/docs`, `app/pricing` (unstyled "Blog", "Docs", "Pricing" headings from the Next template)
- **Category:** Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** A Sanity outage shows an unbranded "Something went wrong!" with no route to phone or WhatsApp. The stubs are indexable and make the site look unfinished.
- **Recommendation:** Build a branded error state with contact fallbacks, and delete the stub routes.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Medical division: products dropdown shows chemical categories; carousel is hardcoded**
- **Location:** `navbar.tsx:233-263` (always renders `chemicalCategories`, despite the comment saying it's non-medical only); `medical-product-carousel.tsx:14-75` (static array, not Sanity)
- **Category:** Usability / Implementation Integrity
- **Impact:** A hospital buyer browsing Clover Medical opens "Products" and sees herbicides and pool chemicals. The medical carousel can advertise items that don't exist in `/products?division=medical`.
- **Recommendation:** Scope the dropdown by division, and move the carousel to a Sanity "featured" query like the chemical homepage uses.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

The code ships a lime accent (hue 127), Archivo/Sora display faces, `rounded-2xl` (16px) product cards and hard-coded `bg-[#111111]` (`utility-bar.tsx:77`), `bg-[oklch(...)]` (`heritage-section.tsx:27`) and inline `rgba(255,255,255,…)` in `products-section.tsx`. ## Patterns & Systemic Issues

1. **"Looks finished, isn't wired."** The form, spec sheets, news articles, brochure and legal pages are all polished UI with nothing behind them. This is the biggest risk to the business.
2. **Opacity as a colour system.** At least 25 instances of `text-foreground/25…/60` and `text-white/40…/70` stand in for tokens. Almost all of the sub-AA contrast comes from this one habit.
3. **Static lists drifting from Sanity.** `config/site.ts` products, `lib/products.ts` (contact page), the medical carousel and the heritage stats each duplicate CMS truth, and several have already gone stale (PPE and Pool 404s).
4. **Desktop-first interaction.** Hover-reveal accordion, hover-pause marquee, a desktop-only Get a Quote and 26–36px tap targets. The mobile visitor, likely most of the traffic in this market, gets the thinnest experience.

## Positive Findings

- **Division theming is well engineered.** One `body[data-division]` switch re-points `--accent`, `--focus` and the derived `--accent-soft` tokens, so nav, buttons, focus rings and chips all follow. That's the right architecture, and it's documented in comments.
- **The catalogue filters are good.** They have a URL-synced state (shareable links), `aria-live` result count, a "Clear filters" plus "Contact Us" empty state, filters that narrow to the chosen division so you never hit a zero-result combination, and a mobile filter drawer with an active-count badge.
- **Forms are labelled properly.** Every contact input has a `<label htmlFor>`, required fields are marked, and there's native `type="email"`/`type="tel"`.
- **Reduced motion is respected** for the marquee and `.product-row` entrance.
- **The hamburger is 44×44 with `touch-action: manipulation`**, the carousel has labelled prev/next buttons, and the theme toggle is labelled.
- **The contact sidebar is strong:** two branches, department-specific emails, WhatsApp and business hours. That's exactly what a B2B buyer wants.
- **Skeleton loaders** for `/products` and `/products/[category]` match the final layout, so there's no layout shift on load.
