## About Us

### Company's History

Clover Chemical Industries (Pty) Ltd ("Clover Chemicals" or "Clover") is a reputable company which has been registered and in business since the year 1980 and is 100% citizen owned company. The company was originally formed to manufacture and supply various chemicals used in domestic and industrial applications. However as business grew the company has expanded and has become distributors of some of the world's leading cleaning multinational brands including Diversey, Kimberly Clark and Columbus, Coopers Shimadzu and Phillips Medical.

Clover Chemicals is also a proud member of the Manufactures Association Board, a proud holder of the certificate of registration for Economics Diversification Drive (EDD) along with certificates in Agrochemicals, Manufacturing (detergents & toilet paper), Radiation, Workshop (service, maintenance and calibration), and Public Health. Clover also holds valuable BOBS standard for BOS 111 – Dishwash, BOS 113 – Disinfectant, BOS 844: 2020 Sanitiser.

### Company's Business Activities

Clover Chemicals provides a much wider range of products that include:
- Manufacture of chemicals and other cleaning solutions and equipment
- Laboratory and medical equipment
- Paper products
- Total floor care solutions
- Agricultural products
- Pesticides, Insecticides, Herbicides, Fungicides
- Protective clothing
